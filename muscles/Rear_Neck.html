Rear Neck

Splenius Capitis

Neck

Overview & Anatomy

The splenius capitis and posterior neck extensors run along the back of the neck, supporting upright head posture and resisting forward pull under heavy loads.

Primary Function
Extends the cervical spine (tilts the head backward), laterally flexes, and rotates the head toward the same side.
Origin & Insertion
Originates from the lower nuchal ligament and spinous processes of C7–T4; inserts onto the mastoid process and occipital bone at the base of the skull.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Prone Harness or Plate Neck Extension

    Lie face-down on a bench with your head clear of the pad, tuck your chin into flexion, and extend smoothly to neutral.

  • Banded Posterior Isometric Hold

    Anchor a light band in front of a rack around the back of your head and hold tall neutral posture against gentle backward pull.

  • Farmer's Carry with Tall Cervical Posture

    Hold heavy dumbbells while keeping your ears stacked over your shoulders to train isometric neck and upper-back bracing.
