Teres

Teres Major & Minor

Back

Overview & Anatomy

The teres major and teres minor sit right above the lats along the outer edge of the shoulder blade, pairing lat-like pulling power with cuff-like shoulder rotation.

Primary Function
Teres major extends, adducts, and internally rotates the arm alongside the lats; teres minor externally rotates and stabilizes the humeral head.
Origin & Insertion
Both originate along the lateral border and inferior angle of the scapula; teres major inserts on the medial humerus while teres minor inserts on the greater tubercle.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown

    Grip slightly outside shoulder width and pull elbows down and slightly outward to recruit the upper lats and teres major strongly.

  • Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown

    Stand with a slight hip hinge, keep elbows softly locked, and sweep the bar down in an arc toward your thighs.

  • Cable External Rotation at Side

    Tuck your elbow against your ribcage and rotate your forearm outward to target teres minor and posterior cuff stability.
