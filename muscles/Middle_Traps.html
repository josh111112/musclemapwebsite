Middle Traps

Middle Trapezius

Back

Overview & Anatomy

The middle trapezius runs horizontally across the upper-mid back between the spine and shoulder blades, giving thickness to the upper back and steadying heavy rows.

Primary Function
Retracts the scapula (pulls the shoulder blades straight back toward the spine) and stabilizes the shoulder girdle during horizontal pulls and carries.
Origin & Insertion
Originates at the spinous processes of C7 through T3; inserts onto the acromion and spine of the scapula.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Chest-Supported Cable or Machine Row

    Use a wide neutral grip, let your shoulder blades glide forward on the stretch, and squeeze them together at peak contraction.

  • Kelso Shrug

    Lean chest-down on an incline bench with straight arms and retract your shoulder blades backward without bending your elbows.

  • Band Pull-Apart

    Hold a resistance band at shoulder height with straight elbows and sweep your arms out until the band touches your chest.
