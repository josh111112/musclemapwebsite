Front Neck

Sternocleidomastoid

Neck

Overview & Anatomy

The sternocleidomastoid and deep anterior neck flexors frame the front and sides of the neck, stabilizing head position under athletic contact and heavy bracing.

Primary Function
Flexes the cervical spine forward, rotates the head to the opposite side, and laterally tilts the neck toward the same side.
Origin & Insertion
Sternocleidomastoid originates at the manubrium of the sternum and medial clavicle; inserts onto the mastoid process behind the ear.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Plate-Loaded Supine Neck Flexion

    Lie face-up on a flat bench with your head clear of the pad, hold a padded light plate on your forehead, and tuck chin to chest slowly.

  • Banded Isometric Neck Hold

    Loop a light band around your forehead and hold neutral cervical alignment against steady forward or lateral tension.

  • Controlled Neck Rotation

    Keep your chin tucked gently and rotate smoothly against light manual or band resistance without jerking the spine.
