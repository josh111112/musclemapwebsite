Abs

Rectus Abdominis

Core

Overview & Anatomy

The rectus abdominis forms the front wall of the midsection, linking your ribcage to your pelvis to transfer force between upper and lower body.

Primary Function
Flexes the lumbar spine, posteriorly tilts the pelvis, and braces the trunk against arching under heavy axial loads.
Origin & Insertion
Originates at the pubic crest and pubic symphysis; inserts onto the xiphoid process and costal cartilages of ribs 5–7.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Hanging Leg Raise

    Posteriorly tilt your pelvis and curl your knees or straight legs upward toward your ribs rather than swinging at the hips.

  • Cable Crunch

    Kneel below a rope attachment, lock your hips steady, and curl your ribcage down toward your pelvis on each rep.

  • Ab Wheel Rollout

    Brace your ribs down and squeeze your glutes as you roll forward, stopping before your lower back begins to arch.

Recovery & ACWR Tracking
