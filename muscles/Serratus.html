Serratus

Serratus Anterior

Chest

Overview & Anatomy

The serratus anterior fans along the upper ribs under the armpit, wrapping around to keep your shoulder blades pressed smoothly against the ribcage when reaching or pressing overhead.

Primary Function
Protracts the scapula (glides the shoulder blade forward around the ribs) and assists upward rotation during overhead movement.
Origin & Insertion
Originates along the outer surfaces of ribs 1–9; inserts along the anterior medial border of the scapula.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Scapular Push-Up

    Hold a straight-arm plank, let your chest sink slightly as shoulder blades come together, then press the floor away to round your upper back subtly.

  • Incline Dumbbell Serratus Punch

    Lie on a bench pressing dumbbells straight up, then push an extra inch toward the ceiling by protracting your shoulder blades.

  • Banded Wall Slide

    Press your forearms against a wall with light band tension around your wrists and glide upward without letting your ribs flare.
