Abductors

Hip Abductors (TFL / Medius)

Legs

Overview & Anatomy

The hip abductors sit along the outer hip and pelvis, keeping your pelvis level during single-leg stance, squats, walking, and running.

Primary Function
Abducts the thigh away from the midline, assists internal or external hip rotation by fiber angle, and stabilizes the pelvis on one leg.
Origin & Insertion
Originates along the outer ilium and iliac crest of the pelvis; inserts onto the greater trochanter of the femur and the iliotibial (IT) tract.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Seated Hip Abduction Machine

    Brace your torso upright or hinge slightly forward, press your knees outward smoothly, and pause briefly at peak contraction.

  • Cable Standing Hip Abduction

    Anchor a cuff at ankle height, stand tall on your support leg, and sweep the working leg out to the side without leaning.

  • Lateral Band Walks

    Hold an athletic quarter-squat with knees aligned over toes and step laterally under steady band tension.
