Hip Flexors

Psoas & Iliacus

Legs

Overview & Anatomy

The iliopsoas sits deep at the front of the hip and lower spine, lifting the thigh during sprinting, kicking, and hanging core work while anchoring lumbar stability.

Primary Function
Flexes the hip joint (bringing the thigh toward the torso) and helps stabilize the lumbar spine and pelvis from the front.
Origin & Insertion
Psoas originates on the lumbar vertebrae (T12–L5) and iliacus on the iliac fossa of the pelvis; both merge to insert on the lesser trochanter of the femur.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Standing Cable Knee Drive

    Anchor a strap low behind your ankle, stand tall on the opposite leg, and drive the knee above hip height without leaning back.

  • Hanging Straight-Leg Raise

    Hold a pull-up bar with braced ribs and lift straight legs smoothly through hip flexion with zero momentum.

  • Seated Straight-Leg Lift

    Sit tall with legs extended on the floor, brace your core, and lift one heel cleanly over a dumbbell or block.
