Spinae Erector

Erector Spinae

Back

Overview & Anatomy

The erector spinae runs as twin columns along both sides of the spine from pelvis to skull, resisting spinal rounding under deadlifts, squats, and bent-over rows.

Primary Function
Extends the vertebral column, resists spinal flexion under load, and assists lateral flexion and posture control.
Origin & Insertion
Originates from the sacrum, iliac crest, and lumbar spinous processes; inserts sequentially along the ribs, thoracic and cervical vertebrae, and skull.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • 45-Degree Back Extension

    Set the pad below your hip crease, keep a neutral torso as you hinge down, and squeeze glutes and spinal erectors up to a straight line.

  • Conventional or Trap-Bar Deadlift

    Brace your lats and abs tightly before pulling slack out of the bar so your spine stays locked from floor to lockout.

  • Good Morning

    Rest a light-to-moderate bar across your upper back, push your hips backward with soft knees, and return smoothly without losing lumbar neutrality.
