Rotator Cuff

Infraspinatus & Supraspinatus

Shoulders

Overview & Anatomy

The four rotator cuff muscles wrap tightly around the shoulder joint like a dynamic sleeve, centering the ball of the humerus inside its shallow socket on every arm movement.

Primary Function
Stabilizes the humeral head in the glenoid fossa while performing external rotation (infraspinatus, teres minor), internal rotation (subscapularis), and initial arm abduction (supraspinatus).
Origin & Insertion
Originates on the anterior, posterior, and superior fossae of the scapula; inserts onto the greater and lesser tubercles of the humerus.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Side-Lying Dumbbell External Rotation

    Pin a folded towel between your elbow and ribs, keep the elbow bent 90 degrees, and rotate your forearm upward slowly.

  • Cable External Rotation at 90 Degrees

    Raise your upper arm parallel to the floor with a bent elbow and rotate the forearm back cleanly without arching your lower back.

  • Face Pull with External Rotation

    Pull a rope toward your forehead while leading with your thumbs backward so the cuff finishes in clean external rotation.
