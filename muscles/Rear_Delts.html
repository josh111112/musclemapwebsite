Rear Delts

Posterior Deltoids

Shoulders

Overview & Anatomy

The posterior deltoid sits on the back of the shoulder, balancing heavy chest pressing, supporting posture, and driving horizontal pulling mechanics.

Primary Function
Horizontally abducts the arm backward, extends the shoulder behind the torso, and assists external rotation of the humerus.
Origin & Insertion
Originates along the lower border of the spine of the scapula; inserts onto the deltoid tuberosity of the humerus.

Top Targeted Exercises

  • Reverse Pec-Deck Fly

    Set the seat so your arms align at shoulder height, keep elbows slightly soft, and sweep your hands outward rather than pinching your shoulder blades first.

  • Rope Face Pull

    Pull toward eye level with elbows flared out and hands finishing beside your ears so the rear delts and external rotators lead.

  • Incline Chest-Supported Rear-Delt Raise

    Lie face-down on a 30-degree bench and lift light dumbbells out at a 45-degree angle away from your body.
