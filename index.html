Frequently Asked Questions

Is MuscleMap Fitness free, and which devices are supported?

MuscleMap Fitness is free to download on iOS via the Apple App Store.

How do I track progressive overload?

Set your target rep ceiling and preferred weight increment, then choose Double Progression (automatically raising weight after you hit your top rep target) or Standard prefill. MuscleMap calculates your next target load from your logged sets.

What is ACWR and how does it help prevent injury?

ACWR (Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio) compares your recent training workload against your longer-term baseline, helping you spot sudden volume spikes before they lead to overtraining.

How does MuscleMap track muscle recovery?

Our interactive 3D muscle map updates fatigue colors in real time based on your logged exercises, and lets you customize recovery timelines and sensitivity for each muscle group.

Can I import my workout history?

Yes. MuscleMap includes a CSV importer so you can bring over your past workout history from apps like Strong in seconds.

Do I need an internet connection to log workouts?

No. MuscleMap includes offline queue syncing so you can log sets, reps, and 1–10 RPE at the gym and sync automatically to the cloud when you reconnect.
